Modular Design Principles

Beyond working and advising on design systems, I have a deep understanding of the core principles behind building components and larger patterns. I have given presentations to teams and can mentor other designers on the same foundation concepts.

Key concepts like separating content from container and the single responsibility principle can be borrowed from Modular CSS along with the notions of portability and scalablity and applied to the work we do in design systems.

People

Collaboration

Collaboration is what makes it all come together. As I have often said, design is a team sport. I work openly by inviting others into the process and transparently by showing my work (including the ugly bits) every step of the way. I use project boards hung on any available wall to show what’s being worked on and give others a chance to respond early on.

Facilitation

I have ran workshops with stakeholders to gather data collectively and gain consensus within a department or organization. These workshops have included people from various positions in the org chart including executive leadership. Additionally, I have facilitated process for teams as an Agile Lead and helped others take ownership of their performance and workflow.

Leadership

I believe that having more experience and advanced technical skill isn’t enough to qualify someone for a senior role. The right candidate must demonstrate leadership skills. This doesn’t just mean taking lead, directing others, or making final decisions. It means putting the team first by helping others and sharing your knowledge and experience through coaching and mentoring.


Ultimately, it’s the people who get the work done. These guiding principles are what drive my daily interactions with teammates and stakeholders. And just being a decent human being.
